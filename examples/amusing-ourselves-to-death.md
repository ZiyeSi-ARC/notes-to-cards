# Example — Neil Postman, *Amusing Ourselves to Death*

Real reading notes (from the Chinese edition 《娱乐至死》), turned into cards
using the `notes-to-cards` format.

---

### Card 1

**Quote**
> "Our politics, religion, news, sports and commerce are all willingly becoming
> appendages of entertainment."

**Idea**
Postman is saying that a medium's *form* changes what ideas can even be said —
television turns everything into entertainment because that is all its form
allows.

**Example**
A Bilibili edit that frames Barack Obama as "the most attractive president" —
a video I would never have searched for, but that the platform served me anyway.
It judged a politician by a scale that has nothing to do with politics.

**Why it matters**
If the medium decides what can be said, then I need to think about what the
*next* medium — short video, then AI — is quietly deciding for all of us.

---

### Card 2

**Quote**
> "The medium is the message."

**Idea**
(McLuhan, via Postman.) The form of communication shapes us more than the
content it carries.

**Example**
Talking to someone face-to-face carries tone, gesture, silence. The same
conversation in text carries only words and emoji — so the medium itself is
changing the message before I even choose what to type.

**Why it matters**
It explains why I care about *where* a message lives, not just what it says —
which is exactly the question I want to study.

---

### Card 3

**Quote**
(My own question, prompted by a teacher's assignment.)
> "If AI overuses quotation marks and separators, will it slowly change how
> people write?"

**Idea**
Postman never saw AI, but his question — does a medium reshape our thinking? —
extends to it directly. AI-generated text is becoming a medium in its own
right.

**Example**
My teacher pointed out the tell-tale signs of AI writing in student work. I
started noticing them everywhere — including in my own drafts.

**Why it matters**
This is the question I could not answer from reading alone, and the reason I
want to study AI and the humanities together rather than as two separate things.
